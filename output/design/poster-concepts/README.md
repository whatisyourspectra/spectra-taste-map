# SPECTRA — Poster directions

Created 2026-10-27 using the built-in image_gen tool, based on the supplied graduation exhibition poster. Exact production prompts are saved in `prompts.json`.

## 01 · Spectral Type

Dark exhibition identity with an oversized gradient wordmark and three enlarged music constellations. Prioritizes recognition at a distance and continuity with the original brand.

## 02 · Taste Atlas

Light editorial map with topographic contours, genre neighborhoods and cover-art nodes. Prioritizes communicating the music taste map concept.

## 03 · Resonance Flow

Dark kinetic composition with a continuous spectral ribbon connecting music nodes. Prioritizes the experience of moving from familiar music toward discovery.
